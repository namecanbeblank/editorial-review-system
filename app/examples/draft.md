# How to build a content review workflow

A content review workflow helps teams catch issues before publication. Start by defining the audience, the purpose of the article, and who will make each decision.

Example Studio is a powerful solution that helps teams streamline their content. The platform makes it easy to work seamlessly across every stage of the editorial process.

## Define your review steps

Give each reviewer a specific job. One person might check the brief and another might verify claims, while the editor makes the final call.
